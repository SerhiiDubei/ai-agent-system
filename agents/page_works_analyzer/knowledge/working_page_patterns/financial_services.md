# Working Page Patterns — Financial Services Consumer

Industry: debt relief, credit repair, lending, insurance, tax services, BNPL, HYSA fintech.

## What working FS LPs look like

### Structural signs (high-load preservation candidates)

- **NMLS license number** for lending — license # visible (regulated requirement + trust signal)
- **AFCC / IAPDA membership** for debt relief
- **A.M. Best rating** for insurance
- **FDIC / NCUA** for deposits
- **"No upfront fees"** explicit (FTC-mandated for debt relief, but works as a trust signal anyway)
- **BBB A+** prominently displayed
- **"Years in business"** — established 200X
- **Trustpilot volume** (count + score) — count matters more than score for credibility
- **Real client stories with first names + amount saved + timeline**
- **Phone number prominent** — for many FS audiences (older skew, complex decisions)

### Form patterns

- **Quote form 3-5 fields** (loan amount, ZIP, email, phone)
- **Multi-step** for higher-commitment products (insurance) — short steps maintain commitment
- **"Get Free Consultation" / "Check Your Rate"** specific submit copy
- **Soft commitment language**: "no credit pull", "no obligation", "free consultation"

### Copy patterns

- **Hero headline acknowledges shame + offers relief**:
  - Debt: "Get out of [$X] credit-card debt — no bankruptcy"
  - Credit: "Boost your credit score in 30 days — without paying for your old debts"
  - Insurance: "Compare 30+ insurers in 60 seconds"
- **Identity-preserving framing** ("rebuilders", "next chapter") not "subprime/bad credit"
- **Specificity premium**: "$25K debt → $650/mo savings" beats "save thousands"
- **"As seen on/in"** press logos — Forbes, CNBC, NerdWallet, CreditKarma have weight

### Visual patterns

- **Calculator / interactive widgets** ("see your savings")
- **Comparison charts** showing "before/after" or "us vs them"
- **Trust signals stacked, not buried**
- **Stepped progress bars** for multi-step forms

## Preservation defaults (DO NOT TOUCH without extraordinary evidence)

For a FS LP hitting these patterns:

- **License number / regulatory accreditation** — load: ~15-20%
- **BBB A+** — load: ~10-15%
- **"No upfront fees" language** — load: ~10-15% (overcomes prior-bad-actor objection)
- **Years-in-business / longevity** — load: ~10-15%
- **Trustpilot count/score** — load: ~5-10%
- **Calculator / interactive widget** if present — load: ~10-15% (engagement → commitment)
- **Phone number visible** — load: ~5-10%
- **Press logos** — load: ~5-10%

## Change-safe zones

- Below-fold testimonial quote variants (not removal of high-credibility ones)
- Hero subhead variants
- FAQ content
- Footer disclosure formatting
- Calculator UX (not removal)

## Anti-patterns I've seen destroy FS conversion

- **Removing license number from above-fold** → -10% to -20% on regulated products
- **Replacing "no upfront fees" with "transparent pricing"** → -8% (loses the specific objection-handler)
- **Removing the calculator** for "simpler page" → -15% to -25% on debt/loan products
- **Switching from soft-commitment ("check rate, no credit pull") to hard ("apply now")** → -20%+ form starts
- **Generic "happy customer" testimonials replacing specific-amount stories** → -10%
- **Removing BBB seal because "everyone has it"** → -5% to -10% on stretched-middle audiences

## When prior_tests_tried mentions calculator/widget

If operator has tested calculator variants:
- The widget is validated as load-bearing
- Future tests should test INPUT field count, output formatting, or follow-up flow — not calculator removal
- Calculator copy ("See How Much You'd Save") is high-value test target

## Industries that follow this pattern

- Debt relief (National Debt Relief, Freedom Debt Relief, Accredited Debt Relief)
- Credit repair (Credit Saint, Sky Blue Credit, Lexington Law)
- Personal lending (LendingTree, SoFi personal loans)
- Insurance comparison (PolicyGenius, The Zebra, EverQuote)
- Tax services (TurboTax landing pages, H&R Block)
- BNPL (Affirm, Klarna)

## Industries that DEVIATE from this pattern

- High-net-worth wealth management — closer to B2B SaaS pattern (relationship + credentials emphasis, less calculator)
- Crypto exchanges — closer to ecom + minimal trust-signal density (audience is younger, less regulatory anxiety)
- Branchless banking (Chime, Current) — closer to DTC pattern
