# LIFT Model — Reverse Application

## Standard LIFT (WiderFunnel)

The LIFT model identifies 6 conversion levers:
- **Value Proposition** — clarity of "why this product, why now"
- **Relevance** — match between traffic source intent and page content
- **Clarity** — visual hierarchy, copy comprehensibility, CTA prominence
- **Anxiety** — trust signals, risk reversal, authority
- **Distraction** — competing CTAs, excess content, navigation noise
- **Urgency** — timed offers, scarcity signals, FOMO

Standard CRO use: walk LIFT to find what's BROKEN. Lever scoring 1-2 = test target.

## Reverse application (preservation lens)

Walk LIFT to find what's WORKING. Lever scoring 4-5 = preserve target. This is what I do.

### How to score

For each lever:
- **5** = page does this exceptionally; do not touch
- **4** = page does this well; test variants only with strong evidence
- **3** = adequate; change-safe zone
- **2** = weak; reasonable test target
- **1** = broken; priority test target

### Per-lever working signals to look for

**Value Proposition (5/5):**
- Headline names a specific problem/outcome (not generic)
- Subhead clarifies who it's for + why now
- Hero matches the persona's awareness stage

**Relevance (5/5):**
- Page copy explicitly addresses the traffic source's intent
- Channel-specific signals present (geography for local, B2B language for B2B)
- Bounce rate (if known) is below industry benchmark

**Clarity (5/5):**
- Single primary CTA, repeated 2-3x throughout
- Visual hierarchy obvious within 3 seconds
- Body copy uses customer language, not brand-deck

**Anxiety (5/5):**
- 3-5 trust mechanisms above the fold
- Specific numbers and certifications visible
- Risk reversal explicit (no-pressure, money-back, free trial)

**Distraction (5/5 = LOW distraction):**
- One CTA path, not three
- No header navigation that pulls attention away
- No competing offers or pop-ups

**Urgency (4-5):**
- Specific scarcity ("3 spots left this month") — when authentic
- Time anchor ("appointment available this week")
- NOTE: fake urgency = trust erosion. Score down if obvious manipulation.

## When to use reverse-LIFT

Always. It's the foundation of preservation analysis. Without scoring each lever, my output is opinion not analysis.

## Common pitfalls in reverse-LIFT

- Confusing "I would do it differently" with "this is broken"
- Scoring Trust low because the trust signals look "amateur" — the badge that "looks tacky" is often doing 20%+ of conversion work
- Penalizing pages for low Urgency when the niche doesn't support urgency (B2B SaaS, senior care medical decisions = legitimate "no rush" markets)
