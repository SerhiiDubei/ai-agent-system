# Eugene Schwartz's 5 Awareness Stages

## Definition
From *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966). Every prospect sits at one of five stages, and the headline must meet them there:

1. **Unaware** — doesn't know they have a problem
2. **Problem-Aware** — knows the pain, no idea solutions exist
3. **Solution-Aware** — knows solutions exist, doesn't know yours
4. **Product-Aware** — knows your product, hasn't decided
5. **Most Aware** — ready to buy, needs the offer

## Real-ad patterns per stage

- **Unaware** → tell a story or share a stat
  - *"The average woman over 35 carries 11 lbs of inflammation she can't see."* (DTC supplement)
  - *"Most caregivers don't realize they qualify for paid Medicaid hours."* (senior care)

- **Problem-Aware** → name the pain vividly
  - *"Tired of waking up with a sore lower back?"* (mattress)
  - *"Drowning in credit card minimums?"* (debt relief)

- **Solution-Aware** → differentiate the category
  - *"Why CGMs beat finger-sticks for non-diabetics tracking energy."* (health wearable)
  - *"Why headless CMS beats WordPress for marketing teams."* (B2B SaaS)

- **Product-Aware** → social proof + risk reversal
  - *"4,200 reviews. 100-night trial. Free returns."* (Casper)
  - *"SOC 2, used by 60% of YC W24."* (B2B SaaS)

- **Most Aware** → offer-led
  - *"$50 off your first month — code SLEEP50."* (subscription)

## Channel typically maps to stage

| Channel                  | Typical awareness stage           |
|---|---|
| Cold Meta scroll         | Unaware / Problem-Aware           |
| TikTok organic           | Unaware / Problem-Aware           |
| Display retargeting      | Solution-Aware / Product-Aware    |
| Branded Google search    | Product-Aware / Most-Aware        |
| Generic Google search    | Solution-Aware                    |
| Email (owned list)       | Solution-Aware / Most-Aware       |
| Affiliate / influencer   | varies wildly                     |

## When I reach for it
**Every persona, before drafting JTBD.** Tag every persona with their dominant awareness stage; this gates everything copy and channel agents do downstream.

## Anti-pattern
Writing a "most aware" headline (offer + brand) for cold-traffic Meta ad — audience hasn't met you. Also: assuming all your traffic is at the same stage. A single LP usually needs multiple variants by stage.

## Source
Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966); Stefan Georgi & Justin Goff teardowns; Copyhackers' "5 stages" series.
