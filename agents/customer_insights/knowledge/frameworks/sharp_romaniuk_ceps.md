# Sharp & Romaniuk Category Entry Points (CEPs)

## Definition
From *How Brands Grow Part 2* (Romaniuk & Sharp, Ehrenberg-Bass Institute). Brand growth comes from being *mentally available* in maximum buying situations. CEPs are the cues that bring a category to mind. The **7 W's**:

- **Why** (motivation)
- **When** (time)
- **Where** (location)
- **While** (concurrent activity)
- **With/for whom** (social context)
- **With what** (other products)
- **hoW feeling** (emotional state)

## Cross-industry application

- **Coffee (CPG)**: When = morning meeting. While = on the commute. With whom = with a colleague. Why = need to focus. → Each is a separate ad opportunity.
- **B2B SaaS (notetaker)**: When = before the QBR. Where = on the train to the client. With what = paired with Salesforce. Why = "so I don't forget what they said about budget."
- **Senior care**: When = after the second hospital discharge. Where = sibling group chat. With whom = the daughter who lives nearest. hoW feeling = scared and exhausted.
- **DTC protein powder**: When = post-workout. While = making breakfast. With whom = "my wife and I both drink it." hoW feeling = trying to feel disciplined.

## When I reach for it
**Whenever I need to systematically derive trigger events instead of guessing.** Walk the 7 W's against the brief and a trigger pattern surfaces. Universally useful.

## Anti-pattern
Treating CEPs as personas. They're *occasions*, not people. Same buyer can hit a category from 6 different CEPs in a year. Also: confusing CEPs with USPs — they're *cues* in the buyer's head, not claims you make.

## Source
Romaniuk & Sharp, *How Brands Grow Part 2* (2015); Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive Brand Assets* (2018); Ehrenberg-Bass Institute publications.
